Hippopotamus

A few words about Hippos:

Hippos are large mammals that live in rivers, lakes, and wetlands in Africa.

They spend much of the day in water to keep their bodies cool and usually come onto land at night to eat grass.

Despite their heavy bodies and short legs, hippos can move surprisingly fast.

They are also very territorial and can be dangerous when they feel threatened.

A few facts:

  • Hippos can weigh more than 3,000 kg.
  • They are mainly herbivores and mostly eat grass.
  • Hippos are one of the largest land mammals in the world.
  • They can stay underwater for about 5 minutes at a time.
  • Hippos have an extremely powerful bite, estimated at around 1,800 PSI, strong enough to crush large bones.
